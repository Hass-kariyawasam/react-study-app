React Study Camp is not just a study tool; it is a complete Career Acceleration Ecosystem designed for the modern developer. Whether you are a beginner writing your first "Hello World" or a pro looking to master advanced concepts, this platform adapts to your pace, lifestyle, and goals.

💡 What Makes This Different?

Most coding apps are boring. We are changing that.

🎮 Gamified Learning: Coding feels like a game. Earn XP, maintain streaks, unlock achievements, and climb the Global Leaderboard.

🔄 Sync Everywhere: Start a lesson on your PC, solve a quiz on your Phone (Android/iOS) while traveling, and code on the Web at work. Your progress is always 100% synced.

📅 Smart Study Plans: Tell us your goal (e.g., "Learn React in 30 Days"), and our AI will generate a custom daily schedule for you.

🔥 Key Features

1. The Learning Hub 📚

Interactive Courses: Step-by-step curriculum starting with React, expanding to HTML, CSS, JS, PHP, and SQL.

Flashcards & Quizzes: Quick revision tools perfect for mobile learning.

Offline Mode: Download lessons on the Desktop app and learn without internet (Guest Mode).

2. Gamification & Social 🏆

Daily Streaks: Keep your momentum going. Don't break the chain!

Global Leaderboards: See where you stand against developers from around the world.

Weekly Challenges: Participate in coding challenges to win exclusive badges.

3. Smart Planner (Coming Soon) 🧠

Custom Schedules: Users can select their target technology and available hours per day. The app builds a personalized roadmap.

Reminders: Get push notifications on your phone when it's time to study.
